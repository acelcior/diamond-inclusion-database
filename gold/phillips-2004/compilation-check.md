# Compilation cross-check: Phillips et al. (2004)

Compares the 80 Table 3 analyses we extracted with the Stachel major-element compilation ([doi:10.7939/DVN/EJUE1G](https://doi.org/10.7939/DVN/EJUE1G), sheet `All diamonds`), whose rows for this paper are cited as "unpubl and Phillips et al. (2004)". Generated by `scripts/compare_compilation.py`; per-analysis results in [compilation-check.csv](compilation-check.csv). Back to [the review page](README.md).

## Result

| Outcome | Analyses |
|---|---|
| matches after V2O3 conversion and 100 % rescaling (≤ 0.025 wt%) | 57 |
| different (> 0.1 wt%) | 10 |
| small differences (0.025–0.1 wt%) | 8 |
| not found | 5 |

**What this tells us:**

- **The compilation does not store the printed values.** It converts V₂O₅ to V₂O₃ and rescales each analysis to a 100 % total. Compilation totals for this paper: 652 rows ≈ 100, 1 rows ≈ 104. So compilation numbers are `calculated_by_compiler`, not `reported`. This matters for D-013.
- **57 of 80 analyses match** the compilation within rounding, once the compiler's two steps are applied. That independently confirms those extracted values.
- **Differences above 0.1 wt% are not extraction errors.** Every extracted total equals the sum of its printed oxides, and the columns were checked against the page images. Those compilation rows hold a different analysis or average of the same inclusion, probably from the online data repository. Both versions are real data. Keep both; resolving them needs the repository file.
- **Not found:** the compilation has no row of that mineral for that diamond (e.g. several olivines, and the DP387 magnesite).
- **The compilation holds much more than the paper prints:** 653 rows, of which 75 correspond to Table 3 columns. Another 59 are further inclusions or analyses of the same 36 diamonds, and 519 are from 396 other diamonds. These come from the online data repository and/or unpublished data. The citation does not say which.
- **The compiler drops the `DP` prefix and leading zeros** (`DP014` → `14`). Linking a compilation row to our observation is a `compiler_derived` identity link, to be approved, never automatic.
- Mineral names used in these rows: `garnet` (308), `chromite` (210), `opx` (81), `cpx` (39), `olivine` (12), `rutile` (2), `Fe-periclase` (1).
