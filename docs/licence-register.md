## What the project shows, for every source

- **Shown publicly:** extracted values (numbers, mineral names, classifications), the citation and DOI, and *where* each value was printed (page, table). Facts are not copyrighted; this is what compilations do.
- **Never shown or shared:** PDFs, figures, page images, or passages of text. PDFs stay in `papers/` (never committed).
- **Stored privately:** short evidence quotes, for traceability. Keep them ≤ 200 characters (the strictest common publisher limit) unless the source is CC BY or CC0.
